What I bring to the role

Research and interviews

I research each guest, write focused questions, and host the conversation, in both one-on-one and co-hosted formats.

Audio and video editing

I edit in Descript and Audacity, cut video in Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve, and learned Pro Tools in my music technology coursework.

Sound and score

I chose and cleared the music across 10 episodes of the Rooted N Human Podcast, and I time music to the emotion of a scene.

Diverse voices

I sourced 137 Hispanic and African American voice-over artists, building lists of voices through direct outreach.

Social video

I developed content plans and produced TikTok and Instagram content for sponsors and marketing campaigns.

Audience data

I read analytics to decide what to change, which led to a series format and a bigger audience.

My story

I earned a B.S. in Information Technology, a world that often felt black and white to me. Film and television carried me through its hardest stretches, and they showed me how culture and the arts can open doors in people's minds, toward accepting themselves and others.

I wanted to help bridge that gap between technology and humanity, for myself and for others, so I earned a Master of Entertainment Industry Management at Carnegie Mellon University. I make audio and video because a clear, curious story can change how someone sees the world.
