Case studies

The work

Marketing plan · Television Marketing & Advertising, CMU

Insecure: launching a story to the fans it was made for

A full TV marketing plan for Insecure, written as if the show were premiering for the first time. It combined audience and market analysis with experiential, on-air, and digital strategy, centered on cultural relevance and representation within the Black community. I led its development.

Why it matters here: marketing a story-driven property with a devoted fandom is the same challenge as marketing a book or an adaptation. You start with who the story is for and meet them where they already gather.

View the plan
Branded content · Branded Entertainment, CMU

Canada Dry: building on a cultural staple

A branded content campaign for Canada Dry, built from the brand's positioning and the cultural conversation around it. I connected the campaign to how Black Americans see Canada Dry Ginger Ale as a cultural staple, then developed content ideas and the creative pitch around that connection.

Why it matters here: the best partnerships grow from what an audience already loves. Finding that insight first is how I'd approach creator and brand partnerships.

View the project
Sponsorship deck · Shades Collective, 2024-2025

Roots & Revelry: pitching partners for real

A sponsorship deck built with a specific partner in mind. It introduced the film and laid out what the partnership could do for the brand and how the brand could help us. This deck was part of the outreach that secured tech and lifestyle sponsorships, alongside tailored outreach emails and follow-up.

Why it matters here: this was live partnership work, not a class exercise. Each pitch was customized to the partner, and each sponsor received a content plan.

Brief response · Illustrative brief I wrote

Meridian: curating to a mood from a written brief

I wrote a creative brief for a film's second act: late-night conversations between a 17-year-old girl of West African and Caribbean descent and her grandmother. The music had to feel like memory, distance, something loved from far away. Then I answered it with an eight-track playlist, with a note on each track's job in the scene.
