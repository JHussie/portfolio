Re: Feature Brief — "Meridian" (Working Title)
Mood playlist request, Act II — late-night intergenerational scene

A preliminary mood playlist for a film's second act: late-night conversations between a 17-year-old girl of West African and Caribbean descent and her grandmother on a back porch. Slow, intimate, emotionally layered. Music should feel like memory, distance, something loved from far away.

Genres welcome: Afrobeats, Afropop, soul, R&B, acoustic, neo-soul, Afro-folk, ambient with melodic elements. Lyrical or instrumental both welcome. Independent or mid-size label representation preferred for clearance flexibility.
